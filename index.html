Formats

A renamed subset, not a full copy of the source font. Custom pair spacing and ligatures are included. TrueType outlines are converted within 1 font unit; WOFF2 is compressed from the TrueType file and comes with a ready-to-use @font-face stylesheet. Autohinting adds ttfautohint's TrueType instructions: crisper text at small sizes where hints are used (Windows, bi-level rendering), about 30% larger files; macOS and most Linux setups largely ignore them. Source hinting and other advanced shaping tables are not retained.
